Nominal GDP growth — initial forecast vs actual, FY1980–2026

At the start of each fiscal year, the government issues an economic forecast; about a year later, the actual figures are confirmed. Zurekei simply records the two side by side, over time.

Reading the gap

The gap is "actual − forecast" — not a verdict. It arises when conditions diverge from the assumptions underlying a forecast: business-cycle swings, disasters, commodity prices, policy changes. A positive gap means the actual came in above the forecast; a negative, below.
