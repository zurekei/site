Indicators

The forecast the government set at the start of each fiscal year, laid alongside the actual figure confirmed later — one page per indicator.

  • Real GDP growth The government's initial forecast (real) laid alongside the confirmed actual.
  • Nominal GDP growth The government's initial forecast (nominal) laid alongside the confirmed actual.
  • Unemployment rate The government's initial forecast laid alongside the confirmed actual.
  • Current account The government's initial forecast laid alongside the confirmed actual.
  • Tax revenue The Ministry of Finance's initial budget estimate for tax revenue, laid alongside the confirmed settlement figure.
  • Government bond issuance The Ministry of Finance's initial budget plan for bond issuance, laid alongside the actual issuance recorded in the settlement. Bonds tracked in separate categories, such as reconstruction bonds or pension special-issue bonds, are not included, following the classification used in the primary source.
  • Total JGB issuance The Ministry of Finance's initial JGB issuance plan (total), laid alongside actual total issuance. Includes construction bonds, deficit-financing bonds, reconstruction and other special bonds, FILP bonds, and refunding bonds (revenue basis, following the classification used in the primary source).
  • Consumer prices (CPI) The government's initial forecast laid alongside the confirmed actual published by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' Statistics Bureau.
