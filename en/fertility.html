Total fertility rate — successive projections vs actual

Fertility assumptions (medium variant) from successive editions of NIPSSR's population projections, overlaid. Lighter lines are older projections; darker lines are more recent.

What this page lays out is the total fertility rate — the average number of children a woman would bear over her lifetime — not the annual number of births, which has its own page.

This chart requires JavaScript to draw. The figures are in the table below.
